Sewing Machine

2026-08-25 · serra · egemen

Let us enjoy this nightmare together, please
I know I'm being obtuse by not taking the hint
that you are kindly trying to refuse my offer
but the tears have become too much, I'm afraid.

Would you happen to know how to sew back a smile?
It is no simple task. First, have the cloth laid:
then
      poke in. poke out. poke in. poke out.
An eternity reserved only for the most vile.

I have brought this upon myself. Well deserved!
The path is either straight to death; or, curved
towards Love's invite: where life can begin anew
in a sweet little bite or their cup of the brew...

though, to survive is not the excuse to love.
No, love itself is reason to snooze the alarms,
for Sun to come around, for a Muse to inspire
reason inside a wandering, blabbering corpse.

Hear the D minor? Go ahead. Poke in, poke out.
The machine only has one purpose for this fever.
Be wound. Be loud. Sew a smile on the cloth.

A shroud is a misfit, a rose shouldn't wither...
but punishment comes and I have to suffer
my needle pointing not in your direction.
