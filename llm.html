About

This is the personal website of Egemen Bahadır Dumludağ, a Turkish lawyer and researcher working on legal engineering, legal AI, and the application of legal methodology to AI safety and political science. He also writes poetry, with a background in English language and literature.

Current work

Egemen has worked since October 2020 as an employment-law lawyer at Rönesans Holding A.Ş. in Ankara. The role covers employment and GDPR advice (documentation, HR policy, employee relations, and rapporteur to the Disciplinary Board); representation in employment and OHS disputes, mediations, and litigation; legal-risk reporting; and commercial contracting (service provision, procurement, software licensing, confidentiality, and rental agreements), plus legal engineering.

He built a Legal Automation project in Python (RPA) that delivered an effective 30% efficiency increase for the team and received Rönesans Holding’s REXA 2021 Innovation award.

He holds an LL.M. in Philosophy of Law from Ankara University (2024; 3.71/4.00). His thesis is Legal Realism’s Renaissance: Legal Decision Making with AI. He also holds an LL.B. (TOBB ETÜ, 2020, double major) and a B.A. in English Language and Literature (TOBB ETÜ, 2019, first rank in class, 3.71/4.00).

He writes poetry and has presented literary work at New Voices, including Sustainable Culture: A Renewable Solution with Faërie (2021) and A Closure to Prophecies: Decline of Science Fiction (2017). In 2022 he presented Yapay Zekâ Etiğine İnsan Temelli Yaklaşımın bir Eleştirisi at Hukuka Felsefi ve Sosyolojik Bakışlar X; the paper appears in HFSA 30 (İstanbul Barosu Yayınları, 2024).
